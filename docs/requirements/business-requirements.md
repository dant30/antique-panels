# Antique Panels — Business Requirements & Phased Roadmap

## 1. Executive Summary
Antique Panels is a specialist automotive garage dedicated to panel beating, computerized chassis realignment, oven spray painting, precision mechanical repairs, and full vintage restorations.

## 2. Phased Rollout Strategy

### Stage 1 — Public Automotive Website (Current Milestone)
- Brand presence, craftsmanship credibility, facility and equipment showcase.
- Structured service offerings with technical descriptions and turnaround estimates.
- Portfolio of documented customer vehicle builds with challenges and solutions.
- Photo gallery with categorized workshop, restoration, and delivery images.
- Intuitive, validated digital booking intake request form.
- Direct contact, location maps, and WhatsApp integration.

### Stage 2 — Customer & Vehicle Management (Phase 2)
- Customer Profiles (Individual & Corporate Fleet accounts).
- Vehicle Registry: Chassis VIN, registration plate, make, model, engine code.
- Appointment Management: Confirmation, calendar slots, dynamic bay scheduling.
- Digital Vehicle Service History logs.

### Stage 3 — Workshop Operations & Job Cards (Phase 3)
- Digital Job Cards: Problem statement, damage inspection checklist, parts allocated.
- Mechanic bay assignments and labor hour tracking.
- Spare parts inventory, SKU tracking, minimum stock re-order thresholds.
- Supplier purchase orders and invoices.
- Invoicing, insurance claim billing, and payment tracking.

### Stage 4 — Executive Management & Analytics Dashboard (Phase 4)
- Revenue and gross margin per bay.
- Mechanic efficiency ratings and turnaround cycle times.
- Outstanding insurance claim payments.
- Real-time parts inventory turnover and profitability metrics.
